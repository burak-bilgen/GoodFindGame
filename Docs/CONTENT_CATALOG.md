# Category content

The V1 catalog contains 50 fictional definitions: 20 gaming/electronics, 15 cameras/lenses and 15 retro/collectibles. Twenty-four typed defects cover eight per category. Fictional model identities stay consistent across locales. Player-facing display names use a short model plus localized product type from the Turkish/English String Catalog; legacy seed names remain searchable for saved-game continuity. Product/brand names remain development fiction and require final release clearance.

Catalog validation rejects duplicate IDs/names, missing categories, empty/invalid prices or defect pools, duplicate defects and cross-category defect assignments. The loader supports future additions without requiring an exact count; launch tests enforce the current 50-definition/24-defect scope and complete coverage.

The complete catalog is separate from the ten-slot feed. Fresh games start with five gaming listings, three camera listings and two retro listings. The first five retain the original starter mapping. Live replenishment rotates model pools and interleaves available categories, including for old saves. Later progression will determine category unlocks; the current category-depth development build exposes all three for verification.

Stored definition IDs resolve items independently of feed positions. Loading an old ten-gaming-item market does not remap its sixth item into a camera. Existing hidden defects remain unchanged, even if a content correction removes that defect from future generation for the model. New headset generation uses connection faults instead of a fan fault; old saved headset truth remains readable.

Defects have category-specific value penalties rather than a universal 30% deduction. The original five penalties remain stable. Combined damage has a 20% valuation floor. Estimates still use revealed defects only; the catalog does not provide an omniscient good-deal label.

Older market calendars decode with an empty scope-epoch list. A content expansion adds the enlarged pools only after the current and next already-known cycle. Past movements, the active movement, queued next movement and already-issued offers stay unchanged. Historical queries use the original pools, while subsequent cycles can target the added categories and models. The migration is persisted and idempotent.

This milestone establishes content, selection, valuation and compatibility. Category attributes/clues, category knowledge, tool-dependent inspection, timed repairs and their economic choices remain the next Days 12–15 work. Progression/category unlocks, rare variants, collections and client content follow in their scheduled phase. The wider market/client/variant/collection template targets and final game art/UI are not completed by reaching the catalog count.
