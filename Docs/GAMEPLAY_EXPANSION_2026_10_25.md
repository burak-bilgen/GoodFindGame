## Shipped behavior

- **Trade notebook:** Sales → Trade history → Trade notebook derives category-level acquisition/inspection/repair costs, proceeds, realized net and profit/loss/break-even counts from settled normal sales and client deliveries. Recent receipts reopen the existing detailed result. Recovery commissions and quick liquidations remain in complete history but are excluded from comparisons. Unknown legacy models remain under Other saved products. Overflow shows unavailable, never a fabricated profit. No new economic balance or save schema was added.
- **Price learning:** Create listing and the expanded sale-price editor show the latest settled normal market sale of the exact same model, with its date and receipt. Client budgets and liquidation prices are excluded. The caption explains that condition and demand can differ. Quiet listings with at least twelve observed views, two minutes elapsed and no open offer get an optional Review price action. It only opens the editor: no price change, buyer reroll or automatic discount.
- **Client sourcing:** after the first sale starts ordinary market replenishment, an active request can reserve one existing arrival slot every third generation for an accessible requested model. Normal seller/condition/price generation still applies, as do shelf capacity, inspection and career locks. The rest of the ordinary model stream advances contiguously so a request cannot suppress the same categories forever. No extra stock or guaranteed bargain is created. Before the first sale, a filtered empty market now explains how to browse starter stock instead of advising the player to wait for arrivals that cannot yet happen.
- **Action priority:** active buyer offers sort by their existing expiry, capped by listing expiry, ahead of recency. Pending replies and stale timers are excluded. Available ready seller quotes sort by expiry. Market handoffs show the actual remaining offer time.
- **Buyer variety:** preserve the introductory identity; later faces use a separate deterministic seed. This does not consume price/personality randomness or change saved offers.
- **Market discovery:** the old single-arrival path reached only 35 of 80 models after 6,000 generations because category and model selection shared modular residues. A phase/cycle rotation with a coprime per-pool stride now reaches all 80, including bounded coverage for partially unlocked pools. Starter slots, reproducibility and the existing 60-batch diversity regression are preserved. This removes unreachable stock; it does not promise instant availability.
- **Offline continuity:** catch-up budgets still bound historical buyer retries, but retain another listing’s already-booked, still-valid offer with its original identity, amount and expiry. Arrival itself changes no cash or goods.
- **Interface and language:** fourteen new strings are complete in all six languages (940 catalog keys total). Notebook receipt controls retain independent accessibility identifiers. Accessibility-size notebook amounts stack below labels to avoid splitting currency values across narrow columns. Six store descriptions and reviewer notes describe the new mechanics.
