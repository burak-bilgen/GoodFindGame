# Category expertise and diagnostic tools

Master brief sections 15–16 require free inspection limited by category knowledge, defect obviousness and tool access. This implements those limits; category-specific numeric attributes and repair services remain separate unfinished requirements.

Each category has independent experience and four ranks. Thresholds are 0, 3, 8 and 16 points. The first self-inspection of an owned item awards one point. A completed professional report awards two points, including when it arrives offline. Booking alone awards nothing. Repeated self-inspection and duplicate event resolution award nothing. Existing saves start with no retroactive experience and retain item truth, revealed faults and paid costs.

Self-inspection is immediate and free. Visible wear is recognizable on items below condition 80, or at rank one. More subtle defects require increasing ranks plus the matching diagnostic kit; see the exhaustive `Defect.detectable` table. The diagnostic fault pass reveals at most 1 + rank previously unknown recognizable faults. Recorded measurements also establish directly corresponding faults (see ITEM_FINDINGS.md), so the report and estimate cannot contradict each other. Discovery never changes hidden faults. Upgraded rank or a newly purchased kit allows a free recheck of previously inspected items without more experience. A professional report remains a paid, timed full defect report, and supersedes self-inspection.

Permanent category kits cost 350 credits for gaming, 500 for cameras and 400 for retro (35,000 / 50,000 / 40,000 minor units). Buying charges once, changes no item facts, grants no knowledge and cannot be repeated. Kits affect their category only. They are an operating expense, not assigned to an individual item's repair/inspection receipt. These are initial tuning values, not human-tested economic balance.

The profile links to a TR/EN expertise screen with ranks, points, category clue guides and kits. Owned items show the relevant rank and can open this screen. Descriptions explain that a clean self-check is not proof of a fault-free item, and that tools still require knowledge. No true market value or unseen defect leaks through these screens.

Persistence adds `inspectionKnowledge` with defaults for prior saves. Loading rejects negative/excessive experience, unknown category keys and invalid inspection capabilities. The maximum rank is three and stored points saturate at one million. Inspection capability records are monotonic per item; existing inspection flags prevent historical or repeated experience awards.
