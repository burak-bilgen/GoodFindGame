# Product art bible — believable fictional objects

## Target

Tactile second-hand objects in one coherent photographic world. Fictional industrial design with usable controls, believable seams, sockets, hinges, materials and wear. Never a logo-erased replica of recognizable branded hardware. No real trademark, controller button alphabet, distinctive real console silhouette or famous camera body layout. General functional forms are acceptable; legal originality cannot be certified by image generation or a visual review alone.

## Masters

Square frame; entire object plus 10–15% margin. Shallow three-quarter view, 25–35 degrees above a neutral warm-gray tabletop; camera lenses viewed slightly above their front element. Soft daylight key from upper left, gentle right fill, grounded soft lower-right contact shadow. Satin polymer, brushed aluminum, textured grips and coated optical glass. Restrained photographic realism, no glowing rarity aura, baked-in UI, labels, text or fictitious unreadable branding.

Condition masters use a 2×2 atlas of the identical design and scale: excellent, good, fair, poor. Good: tiny edge marks. Fair: scuffs, rubbed finish and slight yellowing; poor: heavy cosmetic wear and shallow exterior dents. Wear alone never asserts an internal failure or a missing functional component. Cracks/missing parts require corresponding earned visible facts, not merely low cosmetic score. Package atlas quadrants with the existing packaging script; it crops/encodes generated art without retouching it. Preserve source atlas and generation prompts outside the shipping bundle.

## Scalable presentation

ProductPhoto resolves a model-specific master where available, otherwise a coherent family photograph. Four condition masters remain available for every family. Model identity and item instance identity are separate. Seller photograph direction derives only from saved seller archetype/claims/identity: professional lighting/frame, casual warm room light, careless slight framing angle, evasive tighter crop. Do not choose angle from hidden truth. Keep full useful object framing; never crop away the only actionable known external clue.

Listing appearance reads claimed cosmetic condition, never true faults. Until inspection, owned goods retain the original listing appearance. Inspection switches to observed wear and earned external evidence. Internal battery/storage/sensor faults never become surface scratches. Show a localized evidence annotation anchored to the product presentation for a revealed external finding; do not paint fungus on an unrelated body surface. A lens finding is an evidence detail, not an assertion that the listing photo showed it. Repair removes the corresponding annotation; original report stays in history.

Rare editions are deferred; architecture keeps identity/condition/evidence/presentation separate so an eventual edition can select an appropriate master. No fake rarity colors or value claims now.

## Acceptance

Review full-screen and small feed cells, each wear state, same object continuity, no clipped object, no hidden internal information, no text/logo artifacts, TR/EN accessibility labels and Reduce Motion. Flag implausible generated geometry rather than calling it a real object. Collection pieces need distinct silhouettes; family placeholders are insufficient for a final premium showcase. Document which models have dedicated masters and which remain family-level so scope is honest.
