# Product naming and preliminary identity

22 October 2026. Recommendation for review: **Findrove**. Project Flip remains the internal/bundle/display identity until the owner reviews this working brand. No identifiers, save paths or production metadata have been renamed.

## Evaluation method and limits

Ten deliberately short candidates were assessed for memory, Turkish/English pronunciation, game feel, breadth beyond electronics, visual potential and likely search confusion. Scores are editorial judgments, not consumer testing. Exact-name public web searches were made for all ten; the top three also received indexed App Store/Google Play/Steam checks. This is basic collision triage, not trademark clearance. No trademark registry opinion, App Store reservation, registrar/WHOIS availability, social-handle reservation or domain purchase was performed. Search engines can omit existing products and unregistered rights. Domains/handles remain **unverified for every candidate**.

Scores 1–10, higher is stronger. Discovery is likely brand-search distinctiveness, not a promised App Store ranking. Risk is discussed separately; a great-sounding candidate with a clear collision is rejected.

## Top three

1. **Findrove** — find + rove: the pleasure of discovering overlooked objects, with room for trading and a personal collection. Eight letters, no punctuation/diacritics, flexible across categories. Main concern: Turkish readers may say “findrov” instead of “fayndrov”; test it aloud with both audiences. It needs a trading-game subtitle to explain the premise.
2. **Turnlot** — turn a find into the next opportunity. Clear transactional rhythm, compact wordmark. Main concern: “lot” can imply property/auctions, and it sounds more mechanical than a collection fantasy.
3. **Bidroam** — exploring a market and making offers. Accessible rhythm in both languages and good compass/icon imagery. Main concern: auctions are deferred, so the brand must not promise them.

**Recommended working brand: Findrove.** The emotional association is discovery and ownership rather than a financial utility. Proposed descriptive subtitle for later review: “Trade, Hunt & Collect” / “Al, Sat, Koleksiyon Yap”. Do not treat either subtitle as finalized store metadata.

## Preliminary brand direction

- **Logo:** a slightly slanted price tag enclosing one off-center discovery glint, with a folded corner that can also read as a compass pointer. No currency sign, chart arrow, gavel, bank building or shopping-cart logo.
- **App icon:** one large tactile copper/amber tag, deep midnight field, pale mint discovery glint. Strong silhouette at small size; no wordmark, tiny products or crowded coin pile. The current icon remains shipped until brand review.
- **Typography:** confident human rounded sans for actions/wordmark, compact readable numerals, restrained editorial serif for collection names. The existing native system typography avoids font downloads/license dependencies; an eventual custom font needs its own performance/license evaluation.
- **Color:** midnight #101923, warm copper #D28A4C, amber #F1B85B, sea-glass #7ED6C4, parchment #F2E4CA. Reserve restrained coral for risk/loss. Warm material photography gives the interface a world; avoid corporate gradient-finance blue and hypercasual neon money storms.
- **English tagline:** “Find value. Build your name.”
- **Turkish tagline:** “Değeri keşfet. Adını duyur.”
- **Voice:** observant, concise, quietly ambitious. Status comes from judgment and kept commitments. Products and people have histories; avoid get-rich-quick promises.
