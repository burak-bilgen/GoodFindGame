# Trader career and specialist learning

Four stages: Independent → Known (3 documented inspected sales or one client delivery) → Specialist (8 sales plus category rank 2) → Trusted (16 sales, category rank 3 and three commissions, or 24 inspected sales at rank 3). Loss-making inspected sales count. Item IDs deduplicate trust; no repeat awards for reopened receipts. Trust is a visible 0–100 record at three points per unique transaction; access, never price/speed/fee multipliers, is its purpose. No reputation penalty for a hidden fault, ordinary failed trade or automatically handled shipping.

Budget/mainstream and affordable collector stock form the local market; enthusiast/value-rich collector opportunities require Known, premium requires Specialist. Reserved rare/legendary definitions would require Trusted but none are generated. Initial locked examples preview ambitions; purchase validates access, live replenishment selects accessible definitions. Existing owned goods/quotes are not removed when the career system arrives. Inspection evidence, liquidity, capital and client requirements differentiate decisions; higher base value alone is insufficient content depth.

Original knowledge ranks remain earned at 0/3/8/16. A fourth specialist endorsement requires rank 3 plus six distinct studied models and three diagnosed fault types in that category. Rechecks add no repeat XP. The casebook records only earned findings, including expert reports; repeating cheap clean items cannot satisfy diagnostic breadth. The endorsement narrows uncertain appraisal and public reference ranges; it never reveals a seller's hidden truth. Old saves keep acquired capabilities; optional casebooks default empty, not fabricated retroactive diagnoses. Maximum capability expands to eight to represent rank four plus tools.

Client chains, display ownership and recovery are implemented in subsequent focused commits of this milestone. Game Center, ads, auctions and variant expansion remain deferred.

## Recurring clients

Two contacts remember three authored steps each: Rowan/Ece (retro) and Jules/Baran (cameras). Their names follow the saved market, not language changes. One request can be accepted at a time. Requests do not expire offline; pausing yields no reward or punishment. Later chapters require Known/Trusted status. Final retro request asks for a pristine verified original standard edition, not an unimplemented rare variant.

Delivery requires a full earned expert report, the stated observed condition, no unresolved faults, and original packaging/parts evidence when requested. The engine never rejects an item for hidden truth while accepting inadequate evidence. It refuses items committed to a live sale or report. Matching definitions have explicit substitutes. Leads reference actual current stock; requests never inject a cheap matching listing.

Payout is 102% of observed current regional fair value, independent of acquisition cost/trust. It replaces ordinary sale proceeds; there is no extra quest purse. Settlement records one immutable client sale, removes the item once, credits trust and advances the remembered chapter atomically. Completed chains award recognition/story, not permanent income perks. The six requests form a finite initial career arc; additional months of content are not claimed.

## Two owned collections

Chapter Generations and The Focal Trio each contain three existing models with dedicated product masters. The gallery presents dimmed missing objects, stories, current pieces and a chosen hunt carried back to the marketplace. It is not a daily checklist. Worn inspected examples qualify: a collection is about ownership and story, not requiring every item to be pristine.

Displayed items stay in the eight-slot inventory and retain committed capital. They cannot be sold, serviced or delivered to a client until explicitly returned to trading. This avoids free warehouse overflow and makes collecting a real opportunity cost. Completion requires three different currently owned model identities. A historical Curator distinction persists after selling; the active display becomes incomplete. Reassembly grants no cash, trust or repeated economic reward. No functional cosmetics, passive income or rare variants are introduced.

## Recovery and reward cadence

Recovery preserves knowledge, equipment, collection ownership and career. Ordinary held stock can be liquidated at the lower of 65% of paid acquisition cost and 35% of regional base value. This deliberately realizes a loss, reveals no hidden truth, grants no career credit, and cannot be repeated after removal. Live listings must first be withdrawn; pending reports must finish; displayed objects must be returned to trading.

With cash below $75 canonical, no ordinary trading stock or pending delivery, and a free slot, a local dealer supplies one controller for a basic check. Returning it earns at most $15 service commission, capped at the remaining gap to $75. Supplied goods cannot be sold, collected, liquidated, repaired for advantage, or supplied to clients. They award no knowledge, diagnostic breadth or trust. Ledger records only the service commission, never the consignor's principal. Starting from zero takes five explicit jobs; earning stops at the working-capital floor. This can rescue repeated genuine failures without growing a bankroll beyond the small floor or demanding an ad/payment.

Reward cadence follows actions: useful evidence and ordinary results frequently, a changed client chapter/career capability over several sessions, collection completion and major rank distinctions less often. Selecting a hunt or opening a profile is not an economic reward. No streak, daily mission, premium currency, reward multiplier or timed login obligation exists.

Pre-career saves retain a narrow grandfather set of already-agreed/countered listings. Existing firm agreements can still be purchased within their real validity window; new negotiations use career access rules. Locked opportunity details route to the actual requirements before asking the player to negotiate.

Final presentation review gives the two recurring clients distinct portrait families, and exposes the legacy economy notice directly from the market so existing players can review their conversion without hunting through settings.
