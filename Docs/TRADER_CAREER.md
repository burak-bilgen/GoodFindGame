# Trader career and specialist learning

Four stages: Independent → Known (3 documented inspected sales or one client delivery) → Specialist (8 sales plus category rank 2) → Trusted (16 sales, category rank 3 and three commissions, or 24 inspected sales at rank 3). Loss-making inspected sales count. Item IDs deduplicate trust; no repeat awards for reopened receipts. Trust is a visible 0–100 record at three points per unique transaction; access, never price/speed/fee multipliers, is its purpose. No reputation penalty for a hidden fault, ordinary failed trade or automatically handled shipping.

Budget/mainstream and affordable collector stock form the local market; enthusiast/value-rich collector opportunities require Known, premium requires Specialist. Reserved rare/legendary definitions would require Trusted but none are generated. Initial locked examples preview ambitions; purchase validates access, live replenishment selects accessible definitions. Existing owned goods/quotes are not removed when the career system arrives. Inspection evidence, liquidity, capital and client requirements differentiate decisions; higher base value alone is insufficient content depth.

Original knowledge ranks remain earned at 0/3/8/16. A fourth specialist endorsement requires rank 3 plus six distinct studied models and three diagnosed fault types in that category. Rechecks add no repeat XP. The casebook records only earned findings, including expert reports; repeating cheap clean items cannot satisfy diagnostic breadth. The endorsement narrows uncertain appraisal and public reference ranges; it never reveals a seller's hidden truth. Old saves keep acquired capabilities; optional casebooks default empty, not fabricated retroactive diagnoses. Maximum capability expands to eight to represent rank four plus tools.

Client chains, display ownership and recovery are implemented in subsequent focused commits of this milestone. Game Center, ads, auctions and variant expansion remain deferred.
