Your career, inventory, simulated conversations, inspection knowledge and settings are stored on this device. Your profile name and avatar stay local. Sellers and buyers are fictional characters, not other people.

There are no ads, tracking tools, analytics SDKs, player-account registration or developer-operated servers in the game. Gameplay data is not sent to the developer.

If online Game Center features are enabled, connecting is optional. Apple handles your Game Center account, submitted scores and achievements. The game keeps an Apple player identifier locally to protect result ownership. Apple’s services follow Apple’s privacy policy.

Notifications require your permission and can be disabled in iOS Settings. Device backups may include game data depending on your Apple backup settings. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local game files and recovery backups. Manage Game Center separately in your Apple account settings.

If you email support, the developer receives the address and information you choose to include. Your email provider handles delivery. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. The game does not automatically attach your save or gameplay data.
